More Than Chess

At Outthink Chess, the goal is not simply to memorize openings or win more games. Chess is used as a training ground for learning how to analyze difficult situations, anticipate consequences, solve problems, make confident decisions, and think independently.

Critical Thinking

Students learn to evaluate positions, compare choices, recognize patterns, and make decisions based on evidence rather than impulse.

Focus & Discipline

Chess rewards patience, concentration, preparation, and the ability to stay engaged with challenging problems.

Confidence

Students learn to trust their judgment, take responsibility for their decisions, and recover constructively from mistakes.

Strategic Thinking

Every game teaches students to think ahead, identify long-term goals, and balance immediate opportunities with future consequences.

Chess & Academic Development

Chess challenges students to calculate, recognize patterns, maintain concentration, plan ahead, solve problems, and evaluate the consequences of their decisions. Research has found encouraging connections between chess instruction and mathematical performance in school-age children.

Denmark: Chess and Mathematics Performance

In a study involving 482 primary-school students in Denmark, researchers replaced one of four weekly mathematics lessons with chess-based instruction. Even though those students received less traditional mathematics instruction, the chess group later showed improved mathematics test performance compared with students who continued receiving conventional mathematics lessons.

Read the Denmark study →

Italy: Chess and Mathematical Problem-Solving

A study involving 560 children ages 8 to 11 found a strong relationship between chess ability and mathematics performance. Students who received chess instruction also showed greater improvement in mathematical problem-solving than students who continued with their normal school activities.

Read the Italy study →

These findings reflect one of the ideas at the heart of Outthink Chess: the thinking habits developed through chess can have value beyond the board. Students repeatedly practice concentration, calculation, pattern recognition, problem-solving, patience, and thoughtful decision-making.

Chess Programs

Instruction is designed for young players at different stages of development, from students learning the fundamentals to competitive players looking to sharpen their tactical and strategic skills.

Beginner Chess

Learn the rules, piece movement, basic tactics, checkmates, opening principles, and how to think through a complete game.

Intermediate Training

Develop tactical awareness, calculation, positional understanding, planning, and practical decision-making.

Competitive Development

Training for students who want to improve tournament performance, analyze their games, strengthen weaknesses, and build a serious improvement plan.

Meet the Coach

Meet Bledi Bejo

2170 Peak USCF • 250+ Rated Tournament Games • 2700+ Chess.com Blitz

Bledi Bejo is the founder and head coach of Outthink Chess. A lifelong competitive chess player, Bledi has played more than 250 rated over-the-board tournament games and achieved a peak USCF rating of 2170.

Online, he has reached a Chess.com blitz rating above 2700. His playing style is tactical, aggressive, and creative, with a strong emphasis on calculation, initiative, and finding opportunities others may overlook.

As a coach, Bledi's goal is not simply to teach students openings and tactics. He teaches them how to think: how to analyze a position, compare alternatives, anticipate consequences, make decisions under pressure, and learn constructively from their mistakes.

Outthink Chess was built around the belief that the skills developed at the chessboard can extend far beyond it — strengthening critical thinking, focus, discipline, confidence, resilience, and independent decision-making.
