More Than Chess

At Outthink Chess, the goal is not simply to memorize openings or win more games. Chess is used as a training ground for learning how to analyze difficult situations, anticipate consequences, solve problems, make confident decisions, and think independently.

Critical Thinking

Students learn to evaluate positions, compare choices, recognize patterns, and make decisions based on evidence rather than impulse.

Focus & Discipline

Chess rewards patience, concentration, preparation, and the ability to stay engaged with challenging problems.

Confidence

Students learn to trust their judgment, take responsibility for their decisions, and recover constructively from mistakes.

Strategic Thinking

Every game teaches students to think ahead, identify long-term goals, and balance immediate opportunities with future consequences.

Chess Programs

Instruction is designed for young players at different stages of development, from students learning the fundamentals to competitive players looking to sharpen their tactical and strategic skills.

Beginner Chess

Learn the rules, piece movement, basic tactics, checkmates, opening principles, and how to think through a complete game.

Intermediate Training

Develop tactical awareness, calculation, positional understanding, planning, and practical decision-making.

Competitive Development

Training for students who want to improve tournament performance, analyze their games, strengthen weaknesses, and build a serious improvement plan.

Meet the Coach

Building Better Players and Better Thinkers

Outthink Chess was created to help young players improve at chess while developing skills that reach beyond the board.

Instruction emphasizes active thinking, tactical creativity, disciplined calculation, problem-solving, and learning how to make better decisions under pressure.

Students are encouraged not only to find strong moves, but to understand why those moves work, learn from mistakes, and develop the confidence to think independently.
